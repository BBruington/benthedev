# M6 checklist: cutover

M6 is different from the other checklists: most of it happens on Railway, Neon and Discord with real
accounts, so it follows the runbook, `docs/cutover.md`, part by part. Each step below names the part that
does it and what you should see. Two decisions from 2026-10-14 shape it:

- **The live site uses Beny Bot Dev** for now (SPEC.md §11, question 19). A separate production Discord
  application may come later. While Railway has its token, your Mac runs without it (the same-token rule,
  `docs/cutover.md` Part 4).
- **Railway's own address first.** The site goes live at `https://<something>.up.railway.app`, you run a
  test session there, and only then does your domain move from party-planner.

## Before you start

1. On your Mac, after pulling M6: `mise install` (it fetches Go 1.27.2), then `mise run check` in a
   Terminal tab. It ends with "All checks passed". `scripts/vulncheck.sh` (part of check) now accepts no
   advisory.
2. **The privacy page.** Run `mise run dev`, open http://localhost:5173/privacy without signing in: it
   reads plainly at phone width (narrow the window). The sign-in page links to it under the Discord button,
   and once you're signed in, the menu behind your picture has **Privacy**. Read it once: it should say
   what you'd tell a friend.

## Try it

0. **(Part 0) M6 is on `main`.** The M6 pull request is merged and the GitHub checks on `main` are green,
   before Railway builds anything.
1. **(Part 1) Neon.** A new project with a `colony` database on Postgres 17. Its connection string has no
   `-pooler` in the host, and as entered in Railway it ends `/colony?sslmode=verify-full&channel_binding=require`.
2. **(Parts 2–3) Railway.** The service builds from the repository's `Dockerfile`; **Replicas** is 1,
   the healthcheck path is `/healthz`, **Serverless** is off, **Wait for CI** is on, and the seven variables are in.
3. **(Part 4) The same-token rule.** `.env` has the commented-out token and an empty
   `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN=`; `mise run dev` says `beny_bot=false`. Beny Bot Dev's redirects list both
   `http://localhost:5173/auth/discord/callback` and your Railway address's `/auth/discord/callback`.
4. **(Part 5) First deploy.** The deployment is **Active**; Deploy Logs show "migration applied" for each
   migration, then "Colony Planner is listening" with `"dev_sign_in":false`, `"discord_sign_in":true` and
   `"beny_bot":true`.
5. **(Part 6) Sign in on the Railway address.** **Sign in with Discord** lands you on **All campaigns**,
   the menu has **Site admin**, and your own campaign request is approved at once, with you as its DM.
   `https://<Railway address>/auth/dev` answers "No such route." (no Dev sign-in in production).
6. **(Part 7) Your colony.** The board, People, NPCs, places, quests, patrons, the Mainland (Eldari, with
   your places pinned) and your series match your notes. **View as player** shows nothing hidden.
7. **(Part 8) The test server.** `/link` links your private test server; the next date of your series has
   one event there.
8. **(Part 9) The test session.** A second person joins with the invite link and you approve them; an
   advance reaches their window within a couple of seconds; a request is granted from Discord's **Grant**
   button; a one-off 23 hours out gets exactly one reminder, also after a **Restart** in Railway; starting
   and ending its event stamps the session's colony days; `/colony` and `/nextsession` match the site.
9. **(Part 9) https-only things.** The calendar link works in Google Calendar (it can take Google a few
   hours), and a phone can add the site to its home screen.
10. **CI gate.** Push a small change to `main` after the first deploy (the M6 merge came before Railway
    existed): Railway shows the new deployment **waiting for CI** and deploys only after the GitHub checks
    pass.
11. **(Part 10) Switch day: the domain.** `https://<your domain>` shows the new sign-in page with a valid
    certificate, and **Sign in with Discord** lands you on your campaign as DM. `PUBLIC_URL` in Railway is
    the domain, and Beny Bot Dev's redirects include `https://<your domain>/auth/discord/callback`.
    `https://<your domain>/auth/dev` answers "No such route.", and the Railway address now answers "This
    site answers only at its own address."
12. **(Part 11) The old app.** The old party-planner services are stopped (kept, not deleted) and no longer
    serve the app; the old Beny Bot is offline and removed from your real server; the old recurring event
    is gone. Typing `/` there offers no `/schedule`, `/registercampaign` or `/available`, also after a
    **Restart** of the new service.
13. **(Part 12) Beny Bot in the real server.** The test server is unlinked and the real server is linked,
    with its channels set. The next session has **exactly one** Discord event and gets **exactly one**
    reminder.
14. **Players.** Every player has signed in with Discord at your domain and been approved on **Players**.
15. **(Part 13) Old secrets, now.** The old Neon role's password, the old Beny Bot's token and client
    secret, Clerk's secret key and webhook, the Resend key and the Google OAuth secret are reset or
    deleted, and the new values copied nowhere.
16. **(Part 14) Neon overnight.** With a player's tab left open overnight on a phone that locks, Neon shows
    the compute suspended for most of the night. Another night, with a desktop tab left **visible** on a
    screen that stays on, note what **Usage** added: ______ CU-hours (about 1 expected; about 3 a day on
    quiet days is the trigger for the membership cache). **Usage** shows this month's CU-hours and
    storage; note both here: CU-hours ______ , storage ______ .
17. **One full session** using only the new site and Beny Bot.

## When you're done

- **Day 30** (put it in your calendar): delete the old Railway project, the old Neon project and the
  Clerk application (it holds the old users' profiles), remove the domain from Resend if nothing else uses
  it, and delete the `.pem` and `.env` files in your local party-planner folder (`docs/cutover.md` Part
  13). The old site no longer serves anything, and its Neon credentials, RSA keypair, bot token and
  Clerk, Resend and Google secrets are gone.
- **Local Discord testing** from now on means switching the token as `docs/cutover.md` Part 4 says, away
  from game days, and switching back the same day.
- If CU-hours head past 100 a month, ask for the membership cache (SPEC.md §10 "As built in M6").
