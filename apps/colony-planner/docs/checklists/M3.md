# M3 checklist: quests, patrons, requests

## Start it

1. Make sure Docker Desktop is running. If `mise run dev` is running, press Control-C in its tab. The server has to restart to pick up M3.
2. **The next command erases your local data.** It rebuilds the database and loads the new sample data: quests in every state, Lady Vex with 5 favor, a hidden and an archived patron, and requests in every state, one of them waiting for you.

   ```bash
   cd ~/myCodeSpace/colony-planner
   mise install
   mise run fresh
   mise run dev
   ```

   Wait for `dev: open http://localhost:5173`. The first start after an update can stop while Vite re-bundles the web code. If a page doesn't load, press Control-C and run `mise run dev` once more.

The steps build on each other, so do them in order. A private window counts as a second person; to switch who is signed in there, sign out and use **Dev sign-in** again.

## Try it

1. **What the sample has.** Sign in as **Sample DM** and open Sample Colony: Day 22, Food 63, "19 colonists · 5 unassigned". The **Requests** icon in the top bar shows **1**: Maren's request is waiting.
   - **Quests**: Active has **Clear the Wolf Den** and **Find the Smugglers' Cove** (marked **Hidden**). Done has **Escort the Surveyors**, Failed has **Recover the Lost Ledger**.
   - Open **Clear the Wolf Den**. Under **Failure cost** it says "Only you see this until it fails": Morale −10 and 2 favor with Lady Vex.
   - **Patrons**: **Lady Vex** with 5 favor, **The Hollow Court** (Hidden), and **Captain Orrin** under archived. Lady Vex's page offers **Grain Barge** (3 favor → Food +7), **Hire Mercenaries** (Hidden) and **Spice Caravan** (Archived).
2. **A player asks, you grant (Example G).** In a private window, sign in as **Ana** and open Sample Colony (she is Thorin).
   - Ana: **Patrons** → **Lady Vex** shows "5 favor". On **Grain Barge**, tap **Request**, write a note ("For the winter stores") and **Send request**. It shows under Requests as **Waiting**, marked **Yours**.
   - You: a toast "Thorin requests Grain Barge" appears with **Review**, and the top-bar badge goes to 2. Tap **Review**. The approval sheet shows Thorin with the note in italics, "3 favor → Food +7", Food 63 → 70, Lady Vex favor 5 → 2, and a green **Affordable**. Tap **Grant**.
   - Both windows, without a reload: Food is 70, and Ana's Lady Vex page says "2 favor".
   - Ana: the **Request** button is now off, and under it: "Not enough favor: Lady Vex has 2, needs 3".
   - **Timeline** (both windows): "Thorin called in “Grain Barge” from Lady Vex", "Food +7 (favor −3)".
3. **Two requests, one favor pool (Example G's variant).** You: open **Requests** and **Review** Maren Ashgrove's request (from the sample). The line under it is red: "Not enough favor: Lady Vex has 2, needs 3", and the sheet shows no before → after (spending can't take favor below 0). Tap **Grant** anyway: the sheet stays open and shows the same reason. Nothing changes.
   - Type "Ask again after the harvest." under **Note to the player** and tap **Decline**.
   - **Only the requester and you see a decline note.** Your Requests list shows the note. Ana's Requests list shows Maren's request as **Declined**, with no note. In the private window, sign out and sign in as **Dov** (he plays Maren Ashgrove): his request shows "The DM's note: Ask again after the harvest." Sign out and sign back in as **Ana**.
4. **Completing a quest pays once (Example F).** Open **Clear the Wolf Den** in two tabs of your window. In each tab tap **Complete**: both preview sheets show Food 70 → 90, Morale capped at 100, 3 colonists to Unassigned (19 → 22) and Lady Vex favor 2 → 4.
   - Tap **Complete quest** in the first tab. A toast offers **Undo**, and the quest moves to **Done**. Switch to the second tab: its sheet says "Already resolved", either at once or when you tap **Complete quest**. It pays nothing.
   - Food is 90 (not 110), the board says "22 colonists · 8 unassigned", and Lady Vex has 4 favor, on your screen and Ana's.
   - Timeline: "Quest completed: Clear the Wolf Den", "Food +20 · 3 colonists (Unassigned) · Lady Vex favor +2". Morale was already at its cap, so it isn't listed. A completed quest has no **Reopen**.
5. **Tweaking a grant.** Ana: ask Lady Vex for **Grain Barge** again (she has 4 favor now). You: **Review** it, open **Tweak this grant** and change Food to **5**. The sheet now says Food 90 → 95. Tap **Grant**.
   - On **Requests**, the granted request still says "3 favor → Food +7" (what Ana asked for), with "Granted as Food +5" under it. Food is 95 and Lady Vex has 1 favor.
6. **Adjust favor, then Use action now on behalf of Thorin.** Open **Lady Vex** → **Adjust favor**. Choose **Set to** 10, Reason "Vex hears of the harvest", turn on **Log on the public timeline**, and save. Ana's Lady Vex page shows 10 favor without a reload.
   - On **Grain Barge**, tap **Use action now**. **On behalf of**: **Thorin**. Tap **Use it now**.
   - Tap **Undo** in the toast: Food and favor go back, and both timelines say "The DM rewound a change to the colony" (undo removes the request it made, so the entry doesn't name the action). Use **Grain Barge** on behalf of Thorin again the same way.
   - Timeline (both windows): "Thorin called in “Grain Barge” from Lady Vex", "Food +7 (favor −3)". Food is 102 and Lady Vex has 7 favor.
7. **(Discord) Grant from the test server.** With Beny Bot Dev set up and your test server linked to Sample Colony (`docs/discord-dev-setup.md`, Try it step 10):
   - Ana: ask for **Grain Barge** again. In `dm-desk`, Beny Bot Dev posts "Thorin requests **Grain Barge** from Lady Vex (3 favor → Food +7). Favor now 7." with **Grant**, **Decline** and **Open in app**. **Open in app** opens that request's approval sheet on the website.
   - Tap **Grant** there. A second line says "Granted · Food +7 · favor 7 → 4" and the buttons go. Food and favor change on both website windows; the request shows **Granted** on Requests.
   - Optional: have Ana ask once more and tap **Grant** on the website instead. The Discord message is edited the same way. Tap **Undo** in the website's toast: the message says "Undone" and its buttons come back. Decline it there to clear it.
8. **A failure cost (only you see it, until it happens).** **Quests** → **New quest**. Title **Guard the Harvest**; leave **Visible to players** and **Post "New quest" to the timeline** on; **Patron**: Lady Vex; under **Failure cost** set Food **−200** and **Favor lost** **9**. Save.
   - Ana: **Quests** shows Guard the Harvest under Active, with no failure cost anywhere, and the Timeline says "New quest: Guard the Harvest".
   - You: open it and tap **Fail**. The preview shows Food dropping to 0 (floored: only what's there is lost) and Lady Vex's favor dropping by 9, below 0. Tap **Fail quest**. Lady Vex's favor is now negative: losing favor to a failure may go below 0 (spending can't).
   - Ana's Timeline: "Quest failed: Guard the Harvest" with what was lost.
   - Tap **Undo** in the toast: Food and favor come back and the quest is Active again. Tap **Fail** → **Fail quest** again, then **Reopen** and confirm: the quest is Active, and Food and favor stay where the failure left them. Reopening gives nothing back.
9. **Hidden and archived never reach players.** Turn on **View as player**, then check:
   - **Quests**: no Find the Smugglers' Cove, and no failure cost or DM notes on any quest.
   - **Patrons**: only Lady Vex: no The Hollow Court and no Captain Orrin. Her page offers only **Grain Barge**: no Hire Mercenaries, no Spice Caravan. **Request** is off there ("Players send requests from here.").
   - **Requests**: Maren's declined request shows no note. There is no Review button and no badge.

   Turn **View as player** off. Ana's window shows the same.
   - Open **Find the Smugglers' Cove** and copy its address into Ana's window: "No such quest". Back in your window, turn on **Visible to players** with the post checkbox on: Ana's Quests shows it, and her Timeline says "New quest: Find the Smugglers' Cove", without a reload. Turn the switch off: both disappear from Ana's window.
10. **Campaigns stay apart.** In the private window, sign out and sign in as **Hollis**. Second Sample (his campaign) has The Ridge Council and the quests Hold the Ridge and Map the High Pass, and nothing from Sample Colony. Switch to Sample Colony, where he plays Pell: he sees what Ana sees, with no Review, Grant, Complete or Fail anywhere, and no decline notes.
11. **Phone width.** Make Ana's window narrow, about phone width: Quests, a quest's page, Patrons, Lady Vex's page and Requests fit with no sideways scrolling. In your window at phone width, **Review** a request: the approval sheet fills the screen, with large **Grant** and **Decline** buttons at the bottom.
12. **Checks.** In a second Terminal tab, run `mise run check`. It ends with "All checks passed" after a few minutes. It runs Examples F and G, and the secrecy sweep now plants hidden quests, patrons and actions, failure costs, DM notes, decline notes, a hidden action used and then renamed and revealed, and an undone use of an action hidden since in both sample campaigns and checks that no other player, outsider or other campaign's DM ever sees them.

## When you're done

- Press Control-C in the `mise run dev` tab. `mise run db:down` stops Postgres; your data is kept.
