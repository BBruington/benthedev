# M5 checklist: sessions and Beny Bot

## Start it

1. Make sure Docker Desktop is running. If `mise run dev` is running, press Control-C in its tab. The server has to restart to pick up M5.
2. Open `.env` in your editor and **delete the `DISCORD_GUILD_ID` line**. Nothing reads it any more: M5 removed Beny Bot's one-time clean-up of M1's server-only commands. (Agents never edit `.env`, so this one is yours.)
3. **The next command erases your local data.** It rebuilds the database and loads the new sample data: Sample Colony's weekly "Port Eldari Fridays" game with its last three Fridays played and the Friday after next cancelled, and Second Sample's "Ridgehold Saturdays" every other week.

   ```bash
   cd ~/myCodeSpace/benthedev/apps/colony-planner
   mise run fresh
   mise run dev
   ```

   Wait for `dev: open http://localhost:5173`. If a page doesn't load on the first start, press Control-C and run `mise run dev` once more.

The steps build on each other, so do them in order. A private window counts as a second person; to switch who is signed in there, sign out and use **Dev sign-in** again. Steps marked **(Discord)** need Beny Bot Dev set up (`docs/discord-dev-setup.md`, "Coming from M4") and your test server linked to Sample Colony.

## Try it

1. **The sample sessions.** Sign in as **Sample DM** and open Sample Colony.
   - **Timeline**: the days of three played sessions have a gold left edge, each with a link on its first day: **The Landing** (Days 0–7), **Wolves on the Coast Road** (Days 14–19) and **The Saltmarsh Survey** (Days 19–22). Each has a "Session played: Port Eldari Fridays" entry on its first day that links to the session.
   - Open **The Saltmarsh Survey**: last Friday's date at 8:00 PM, "Days 19–22" under "In the colony", and its recap ("The party mapped the saltmarsh…"). **See it on the timeline** opens the timeline at Day 19.
   - The **Colony** board has a card with the next game night and the last recap's first lines.
2. **A player sees the same.** In a private window, sign in as **Ana** and open Sample Colony.
   - **Sessions** lists the upcoming Fridays, the Friday after next struck through as cancelled, and the history with recaps and "Days 19–22" and so on. Her only button is **Copy** on the calendar link: no Cancel, Mark played or Schedule.
   - The session pages show the recaps as plain text, with no controls.
   - **NPCs** → **Brannoc**: "First met" says **The Landing** and its date, linking to that session.
3. **Friday 8:00 PM New York across the clock change.** In your window, **Settings**: the time zone is America/New York. **Sessions** → **Port Eldari Fridays** → **Edit**: weekly, Friday, 8:00 PM, 4 hours.
   - The upcoming list shows every Friday for the next 12 weeks. Each is 8:00 PM table time: **8:00 PM EDT** through Friday, October 30, and **8:00 PM EST** from **Friday, November 6** (the first Friday after the November 1 clock change) onward. If your Mac isn't on New York time, your own time comes first and a second line says "Table time 8:00 PM EDT (New York)" (EST from November 6), in Ana's window too (players read the time zone on the board). Her played sessions show their table time as well.
   - To try it from scratch: **Schedule** → title "DST check", **Every week**, **Friday**, first date this Friday, 8:00 PM, save. It lists the same 8:00 PM Fridays. Delete it afterwards.
   - Every other week counts whole weeks from the first date: **Schedule** → "Weekend check", **Every other week**, **Saturday** and **Sunday**, first date this Saturday. It lists this Saturday and the Sunday after it, then the pair two weeks later, and so on. Delete it afterwards.
4. **Cancel and restore.** Cancel next Friday on **Sessions**: it's struck through with a "Cancelled" badge in both windows, without a reload, and the board's next game night moves to the Friday after it. **Restore** it: it's back as the next one.
5. **Played, ended, stamped.** **Sessions** → **Schedule**: title "The Tide Mill", **One-off**, today's date, a start an hour from now, 3 hours, with the **Discord event** and **Reminder** switches **off** (so it doesn't disturb the Discord steps), save. It's the next date, so it has **Mark played**: click it. Its page opens, marked played, "In the colony" from today's day (Day 22). Go to the board and **Advance** +1. Back on the session, **End session**: "Days 22–23". **Rename** it "The Tide Mill, part one", write a recap, save. With the stamp editor set the days to 23–23: on the **Timeline**, its "Session played" entry moves to Day 23 with the band. Set them back to 22–23: Days 22–23 have the gold edge, "Session · The Tide Mill, part one · Days 22–23", and the entry is back on Day 22. Ana's window shows it all, recap included.
6. **First met.** **NPCs** → any revealed NPC (Lady Vex) → **First met** picker: choose **The Tide Mill, part one**. It saves at once. Ana's window shows it under "First met" on Lady Vex's page. Turn on **View as player**: the page shows the fact and no picker. Turn it off.
7. **Event location and calendar link.** **Settings** → Discord card: the **Event location** says "Online" (every campaign's default); change it to "Foundry, table 2" and save. The **Calendar link** card shows the link with **Copy** and **New link…**.
8. **Apple Calendar.** **Copy** the calendar link. In Calendar on the Mac, **File → New Calendar Subscription…**, paste it and **Subscribe** (if Calendar refuses the address, change `http://` at its start to `webcal://`). Set **Auto-refresh** to every 5 minutes and **OK**.
   - Every upcoming Friday appears at 8:00 PM New York time (shown in your Mac's zone), 4 hours long, titled **Port Eldari Fridays**, at "Foundry, table 2". The cancelled Friday after next is missing; the played Fridays of the past 30 days and The Tide Mill are there (the feed keeps played dates and drops only cancelled ones).
   - Cancel next Friday on **Sessions**, then **View → Refresh Calendars** in Calendar: it disappears. Restore it and refresh: it's back.
   - **Settings** → **New link…** → confirm: refreshing the old subscription in Calendar now fails (the old link answers "No such route"). Delete the old subscription and subscribe to the new link if you want to keep it.
9. **(Discord) Events and the announcement.** On **Settings**, pick an **Announce channel** and a **Reminder channel**, and save. Within a few seconds, **Events** at the top of your test server's channel list shows **Port Eldari Fridays** next Friday at **8:00 PM** New York time (Discord shows it in your zone), at "Foundry, table 2". The announce channel shows "**Port Eldari Fridays** is scheduled for *date* (*in N days*)". Restart `mise run dev`: there is still exactly one event.
10. **(Discord) Cancel and restore.** Cancel next Friday on **Sessions**.
    - Its Discord event disappears; "❌ **Port Eldari Fridays** on *date* has been cancelled." appears in the reminder channel; and an event for the **following** Friday appears within a few seconds (the cancelled Friday after next is skipped, so it's the next uncancelled one).
    - **Restore** next Friday: the later event goes away and next Friday's event is back.
    - Mark someone **Interested** in next Friday's event. **Sessions** → **Port Eldari Fridays** → **Edit**: change **Friday** to **Saturday** and save. Within a few seconds the same event moves to next Saturday, still with its Interested count. Change it back to Friday.
    - **Settings** → **Event location** "Foundry, table 3", save: the existing event's location changes within a few seconds. Put it back to "Foundry, table 2".
11. **(Discord) One reminder for a one-off 23 hours out.** **Sessions** → **Schedule**: title "One-off test", **One-off**, the date and time 23 hours from now in New York time (the form's time is labelled with the table's zone), 2 hours, both switches on, save.
    - Within a few seconds the reminder channel shows "Reminder: **One-off test** is coming up *in 23 hours* (*date*). Don't forget!", **once**, and **Events** shows the one-off's event.
    - Press Control-C in the `mise run dev` tab and run `mise run dev` again. Wait a minute: **no second reminder**.
12. **(Discord) Start and end the event.** In Discord, open **One-off test**'s event and click **Start Event** (Discord lets you start it early).
    - Within a few seconds its session page on the website says played, "In the colony" from today's day.
    - On the board, **Advance** +1. In Discord, end the event. The session page shows "Days 23–24", the **Timeline** shows its band, and a "Session played" entry links to it.
    - An event you make by hand in Discord and start does nothing.
13. **(Discord) `/colony` and `/nextsession`.** In your test server, type `/colony`: only you see an embed with the colony's name (linking to the site), the day, the stockpile, colonists with Unassigned, the next shipment and the next session. It matches the **Colony** board in View as player. `/nextsession` says "The next session, **Port Eldari Fridays**, starts *date* (*in N days*)." with the same date as **Sessions**. Allow a minute for Discord to offer the two new commands.
14. **(Discord) Unlink.** On **Settings**, **Unlink…** your test server and confirm: within a few seconds Beny Bot Dev's upcoming events there are gone. Link it again with a new code (`/link`) and pick the channels again (unlinking clears them): the next Friday gets a fresh event there.
15. **Campaigns stay apart.** Sign in as **Hollis** and open **Second Sample**: **Sessions** shows **Ridgehold Saturdays** every other Saturday at 7:00 PM (London time), with **Up the Mule Track** played over Days 0–9. Nothing from Sample Colony, and his calendar link is a different one.
16. **Checks.** In a second Terminal tab, run `mise run check`. It ends with "All checks passed" after a few minutes. The secrecy sweep now plants a series, a played session with a recap and both NPCs' first session in both sample campaigns, checks that no outsider or other campaign's DM ever sees them or the campaign's calendar token, and that one campaign's calendar never shows another's sessions.

## When you're done

- Delete the "One-off test" and any "DST check" series on **Sessions** if you like (played sessions stay in the history), and unsubscribe in Calendar.
- Press Control-C in the `mise run dev` tab. `mise run db:down` stops Postgres; your data is kept.
