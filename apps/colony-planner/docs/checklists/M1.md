# M1 checklist: colony clock and books

## Start it

1. Make sure Docker Desktop is running.
2. If `mise run dev` is already running from M0, press Control-C in its tab first. The server has to restart to pick up M1 and its new database migration.
3. In Terminal:

   ```bash
   cd ~/myCodeSpace/colony-planner
   mise install
   mise run fresh
   mise run dev
   ```

   `fresh` wipes the local database, applies the new M1 migration and loads the sample campaign with its sample colony. Wait for `dev: open http://localhost:5173`.

The steps below build on each other, so do them in order. If something goes wrong partway, `mise run fresh` (while dev keeps running) starts the sample colony over; everyone signs in again.

Food runs out quickly in the sample colony. That is on purpose: food stops at 0 and nothing else happens. The app keeps the books; you are the engine.

## Try it

Sign in as **Sample DM** in your normal window. A private window counts as a second person.

1. **The sample colony.** The Colony page shows "Port Eldari" with:
   - the day dial at **Day 22**, and under it "Day 22 · next ship Day 28, in 6 days";
   - Gold 210, Food 63, Materials 160, Morale 100 (out of 100);
   - "19 colonists · 5 unassigned", with a chip for each role;
   - "Latest from the timeline", where "Stock adjusted … Paid the smuggler at the north cove" carries **Hidden from players**.

   Open **Timeline**. It opens on "Days 22–28" with today ringed. Use the arrow to go to "Days 15–21": the smuggler entry on Day 19 has the eye-slash mark. Going back to the first page shows the founding on Day 0.
2. **A player sees the same books.** In a private window, sign in as **Ana (Thorin)**.
   - The top bar and page title say "Port Eldari", not "Colony Planner".
   - She sees the same day, stock and colonists, but no Advance, Adjust, Shipments, Correct day or Undo.
   - Her Timeline has no smuggler entry on Day 19.

   Keep Ana's window open and visible next to yours for the next step.
3. **Live at the table.** In your DM window, tap **+3**. A preview sheet shows "Day 22 → 25", Food 63 → 6 and "No shipment arrivals". Tap **Advance 3 days**.
   - Your board shows Day 25 and Food 6 with a "−57" chip, and one toast with an **Undo** button.
   - Within about 2 seconds, without reloading, Ana's window shows the toast "3 days pass · Day 25" and the same −57 chip on Food.

   A window that is hidden (minimised, or a background tab) stops listening on purpose and catches up when you show it again.
4. **Undo.** On your board, under the clock, "Last change: 3 days pass · Day 25" has an **Undo** button. Tap it, read the dialog that names the change, and tap **Undo**.
   - Everything is back: Day 22, Food 63, next ship Day 28.
   - The box now says there is nothing to undo.
   - On the Timeline, the undone entry is struck through for you. Ana sees "The DM rewound: 3 days pass · Day 25" instead.
5. **Shipments every 7 days (Example A).** Tap **Shipments**. Set "Days between ships" to **7** and "Next arrival day" to **29**, then **Save schedule**. The clock says "next ship Day 29, in 7 days".
   - Tap **Custom**, type **6**. The preview says "Day 22 → 28", Food 63 → 0 "(at the minimum)", "No shipment arrivals". Advance. The clock says "Day 28 · next ship Day 29, in 1 day".
   - Tap **+1**. The preview says "1 shipment arrival (Day 29)". Advance. The clock says "Day 29 · next ship Day 36, in 7 days". The Timeline has "Shipment arrived · Day 29" on Day 29.
6. **Every advance is previewed, and the result matches.** In steps 3 and 5, check that the day, food and arrival days the preview promised are what the board and Timeline show afterwards.
7. **Undo offers only the newest change.** The box under the clock now says "Last change: 1 day passes · Day 29". The 6-day advance before it can no longer be undone. Leave it.
8. **One million days (Example H).** Tap **Custom** and type **1000000**.
   - The preview says "Day 29 → 1,000,029" and "142,857 shipment arrivals (Days 36 – 1,000,028, every 7)".
   - A "Type the number again" field appears, and the Advance button stays disabled until you type 1,000,000 there.
   - Advance. The Timeline opens on the new page with exactly two new entries: "142,857 shipments arrived, every 7 days, Days 36 – 1,000,028" and "1,000,000 days pass · Day 1,000,029".
   - Undo it (toast or the box under the clock). The colony is back on Day 29, Food 0, next ship Day 36.
9. **Double confirm (Example E).** Open the Colony page in a second tab of your DM window. In both tabs tap **+1** so both previews are open. Tap **Advance 1 day** in the first tab, then in the second.
   - The colony moves one day only (Day 30).
   - The second tab's sheet says "The colony changed (now Day 30). Review and retry." and its preview refreshes.

   Close the second tab's sheet with Cancel. Clicking Advance twice very quickly in one tab also changes the colony only once.
10. **Record cargo.** Open **Timeline**, find "Shipment arrived · Day 29" (use the left arrow if needed), open its ⋮ menu and choose **Record cargo…**.
    - Enter Food **+50** and New colonists **3**. The preview at the bottom reads "Food +50 · 3 colonists (Unassigned)". Tap **Record cargo**.
    - The board shows Food 50 and "22 colonists · 8 unassigned".
    - The Timeline has "Cargo from the Day 29 shipment", which names the arrival it came from.
11. **Correct the day (Example D5).** On the Colony page tap **Correct day**, replace the day with **45** and tap **Correct the day**.
    - The clock says "Day 45 · next ship Day 50, in 5 days". Food is unchanged: no upkeep runs.
    - The Timeline has "Day 30 → 45 (2 shipment dates skipped)", marked Hidden from players. Ana's Timeline doesn't show it.
12. **Clear the schedule, then edit food.** Tap **Shipments**, then **Clear schedule**. The clock says "Day 45 · no ships scheduled" and the countdown is gone.
    - Tap **Adjust stock**. For Food choose **Set to** and type **120**. The preview says "Food +70". Tap **Adjust stock**. Food shows 120.
    - The entry is hidden from players unless you turn on "Log on the public timeline".
13. **View as player.** Turn on **View as player** (the eye button at the top; on a wide window, the "View as player" switch). A banner appears.
    - The board has no Advance chips, Adjust stock, Shipments, Correct day or Undo.
    - The Timeline has no Hidden from players entries (the smuggler, the day correction, the food adjustment).
    - Turning it on doesn't bring back toasts for old entries.
    - Timeline pages you opened before turning it on don't show the hidden entries, not even for a moment.

    Tap **Back to DM view**.
14. **Notes on the timeline.** On the Timeline, tap **+** on any day, give the entry a title and **Save entry**. It appears on that day, and Ana's window toasts it. From its ⋮ menu you can **Edit** it or **Delete…** it. Entries the app wrote can be edited (text and visibility) but not deleted.
15. **Resources.** Open **Settings**. Under **Resources**:
    - **Add a resource** named Timber with any icon. It appears on everyone's board at 0.
    - Adding another resource named "timber" is refused.
    - Editing Gold with a maximum of 100 is refused while Gold is above 100.
    - Food can't be archived. Archiving Timber removes it from the board.

    The Colony name field is here too. Changing it changes the top bar for everyone.
16. **Checks.** In a second Terminal tab, run `mise run check`. It ends with "All checks passed" after a few minutes. It includes the rules examples A–I from the spec, and tests that an idle open tab and a reconnect run no database query.

## Optional

17. **On a phone.** Run `mise run dev:phone` instead of `mise run dev` and open the address it prints on a phone on the same Wi-Fi. The board, the sheets and the Timeline work one-handed and never scroll sideways.
18. **Reduced motion.** With "Reduce motion" turned on in macOS (System Settings › Accessibility › Display), stock numbers change at once instead of counting up.
19. **Resource order.** There is no way to reorder resources yet. New ones go to the end.
20. **Keyboard.** Tab to **+1** and press Enter: the sheet opens on the days field. Press Escape: focus is back on **+1**. Cancel, a confirmed advance and the other sheets and dialogs do the same. One opened from an entry's ⋮ menu returns to that ⋮ button.

## When you're done

- Press Control-C in the `mise run dev` tab.
- `mise run db:down` stops Postgres. Your data is kept.
