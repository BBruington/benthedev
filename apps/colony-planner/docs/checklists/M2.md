# M2 checklist: people and secrecy

## Start it

1. Make sure Docker Desktop is running. If `mise run dev` is running, press Control-C in its tab. The server has to restart to pick up M2.
2. **The next command erases your local data.** It rebuilds the database and loads the new sample data: NPCs (some hidden), places, and named colonists such as Brannoc the farmer.

   ```bash
   cd ~/myCodeSpace/colony-planner
   mise install
   mise run fresh
   mise run dev
   ```

   Wait for `dev: open http://localhost:5173`. The first start after an update can stop while Vite re-bundles the web code. If a page doesn't load, press Control-C and run `mise run dev` once more.

The steps build on each other, so do them in order. A private window counts as a second person.

## Try it

1. **The board and People agree.** Sign in as **Sample DM** and open Sample Colony: Port Eldari, Day 22, Food 63, and the colonists strip says "19 colonists · 5 unassigned". Tap the strip (or **People** in the menu).
   - The Roles table shows Unnamed, Named and Total for each role: Farmer 3 + 1 = 4 (Brannoc), Healer 0 + 1 = 1 (Sister Maren), Builder 2 + 1 = 3 (The Quiet Mason), and Unassigned 5, highlighted because people are waiting there.
   - The **Colonists** row says 19, the same as the board.
   - Under **Named colonists**, The Quiet Mason shows "True name Edric Vael" and a **Hidden** badge. Only you see either.
2. **Moving people.** Tap **Transfer**. Choose From **Unassigned**, To **Farmer**, How many **6**. The sheet says "Unassigned has only 5 unnamed colonists, so 6 can't move." and the **Transfer** button stays off.
   - Change it to **3** and tap **Transfer**. A toast says "3 colonists: Unassigned → Farmer", with **Undo**.
   - Farmer is now 7 and Unassigned 2. Colonists is still 19, and the board says "19 colonists · 2 unassigned".
   - On **Timeline** the entry has the eye-slash: only you see it, because **Post publicly** was off.
3. **Setting a count.** Tap **Set count**, pick **Soldier** and set it to **3**. Colonists goes to 20. This is how you record deaths and departures. Tap **Undo** in the toast: back to 19.
4. **A new NPC joins as a new colonist.** Open **NPCs** → **Add an NPC**. Name them **Wynn Hollow**. The **Visible to players** switch starts off: new NPCs start hidden. Save. You land on Wynn Hollow's page, marked **Hidden**.
   - On **People**, tap **Join colony**. NPC **Wynn Hollow**, Role **Farmer**, How **A new colonist** → **Join as Farmer**.
   - Colonists goes from 19 to 20, on People and on the board. Farmer's Named column goes up by one.
   - The timeline entry "Wynn Hollow joined the colony as Farmer" is DM-only, because Wynn is hidden.
5. **Promoting someone already there.** Tap **Join colony** again. NPC **Pip Lark**, Role **Farmer**, How **Promote an unnamed colonist** → join. Colonists stays at 20: Farmer's Unnamed goes down by one and Named goes up by one.
6. **Changing a role.** On Pip Lark's row, tap **Role** and pick **Scholar**. Farmer goes down by one and Scholar up by one. Colonists stays at 20.
7. **A colonist can't be deleted.** Open Wynn Hollow's page (NPCs → Wynn Hollow) and tap **Delete**. It is refused: "Wynn Hollow is a colonist. Remove them from the colony first." Nothing is deleted.
   - Tap **Leave colony** → **Remove from the colony**. Colonists drops to 19. Tap **Undo** in the toast: 20 again, and Wynn is a Farmer again.
   - Tap **Leave colony** → **Remove from the colony** again, then **Delete**. This time Wynn Hollow is deleted.
   - (**Return to the pool** is the other choice: the NPC stops being named, and their role keeps the headcount as one more unnamed colonist.)
8. **Dead changes no numbers.** Open **Brannoc** → **Edit** → Status **Dead** → save. Colonists doesn't change. His page says "Marked dead, but still counted as a colonist. Remove them from the colony if they're gone." Nothing happens on its own; leave him as he is or remove him, your choice.
9. **Roles in Settings.** Open **Settings** → **Colonist roles**.
   - **Add a role**: name it **Fisher**, pick an icon, save. Fisher appears at the end of the table on People, with 0.
   - **Edit** Unassigned: "Unassigned keeps its name.", and the Archived switch is off and locked ("New colonists land here, so it stays."). You can still change its icon and position.
   - **Edit** Farmer: Archived is locked ("… colonists in this role. Move them out first.").
   - **Edit** Fisher: turn on **Archived** and save. Fisher disappears from the board and People.
   - **Position** decides the order: lower numbers come first.
10. **Places.** Open **Places**: five places, and **The Drowned Chapel** has a **Hidden** badge. Open **Port Eldari Docks**: "NPCs who are here" lists Orsolya Dane and The Hooded Stranger. **Add a place**: its **Visible to players** switch starts on. New places start visible. Saving opens the new place's page. Delete it again if you like.
11. **What players see (View as player).** Turn on **View as player**, then check:
    - **NPCs**: seven NPCs. Mother Corrow, The Quiet Mason and The Wolf-Father appear nowhere. The Hooded Stranger appears under that name only, with no "True name" line and no DM notes.
    - **Search**: `Selwyn`, `Edric`, `Hrothgar` and `Corrow` find nobody. `stranger` finds The Hooded Stranger through his alias.
    - **People**: Builder still totals 3, but The Quiet Mason isn't listed under Named colonists. Colonists still equals the board.
    - **Places**: The Drowned Chapel is gone. **Wolf Den Hollow** says "Nobody the party knows is here.", because The Wolf-Father is hidden.
    - **Timeline**: no entry names The Quiet Mason or Wynn Hollow.

    Turn **View as player** off.
12. **A real player sees the same.** In a private window, sign in as **Ana** and open Sample Colony (she is Thorin). Repeat the checks in step 11: everything matches.
    - In your window, open The Quiet Mason's page and copy its address. Paste it into Ana's window: "No such NPC".
    - Make the window narrow, about phone width: NPCs, People and Places still fit, with no sideways scrolling.
13. **Revealing is live.** In your window, open **The Quiet Mason** and turn on **Visible to players**. Ana's NPCs page and People page show The Quiet Mason without a reload, and never "Edric Vael". Turn the switch off again: he disappears from Ana's pages.
    - Open **Pip Lark** → **Edit**, set **Known name** to **The Lark** and save. Ana's Timeline no longer shows "Pip Lark joined the colony as Farmer": it names him by a name the party no longer uses, so it became DM-only. Switching it back to public is refused until you edit its text.
14. **Campaigns stay apart.** In another private window, sign in as **Hollis** and open Second Sample, which he runs. NPCs shows Foreman Dagny, Old Brisk, The Lantern Man (true name Aldo Pike) and The Thing Below (Hidden), and nobody from Sample Colony. Switch to Sample Colony, where he plays Pell: he sees exactly what Ana sees, with no DM controls.
15. **(Discord) `/npc view`.** With Beny Bot Dev set up and your test server linked to Sample Colony (`docs/discord-dev-setup.md`, Try it step 7):
    - Type `/npc view` and start typing in the `name` box. The suggestions include The Hooded Stranger, and never The Quiet Mason or anyone else hidden.
    - Pick The Hooded Stranger. The reply shows what players see (alias, race, status, where he is) and never "Selwyn Hart" or DM notes, even though you are the DM. Discord marks it "Only you can see this".
    - Type `Edric Vael`, `Selwyn Hart` or `The Quiet Mason` and send. Each answers "No NPC the party knows goes by that name.", again only to you.
16. **Checks.** In a second Terminal tab, run `mise run check`. It ends with "All checks passed" after a few minutes. It includes the secrecy sweep, which now plants hidden NPCs, a hidden place, a hidden named colonist, true names and DM notes in both sample campaigns and checks that no player, outsider or other campaign's DM ever sees them.

## When you're done

- Press Control-C in the `mise run dev` tab. `mise run db:down` stops Postgres; your data is kept.
